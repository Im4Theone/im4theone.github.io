About

I don't separate design from engineering.

How something looks and how it works are the same decision to me. I'll sketch an interface and wire its backend in the same sitting, because treating them as separate jobs just means fixing the seam between them later.

Most of what I build lives across the web stack — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript at the surface, Python, Java, and C# / .NET underneath — plus a long-running habit of shipping small tools for Minecraft servers, where a few hundred players notice immediately if something feels off.
